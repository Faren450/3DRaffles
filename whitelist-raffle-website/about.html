How We Began

    What started 3DRaffles was born out of genuine, real-world pain points.
  • Clunky technology,
  • Terrible customer support,
  • Unfair pricing, and
  • Delayed data recovery.

I was just plain pissed off by the other guys platforms that were stupidly frustrating, overly confusing, massively disorganized, horridly lacking in support and worse of all, hugely over priced so I decided to do something about it.

What I wanted was a platform that actually respects the user's time and business needs so I created 3DRaffles

Our Mission Statement:

At 3DRaffles, our mission is to democratize high-impact marketing by stripping away the complexity, exorbitant costs, and neglect typical of this specialized software industry. We believe that raffles are an elite tool for hyper-targeted audience growth, but the platforms powering them shouldn't require an advanced tech degree, leave you stranded without support or require a kings ransom to use.

We built 3DRaffles on a foundation of radical accessibility, real-time data independence, and human-first technical support at a price the world can afford.

Our promise is simple: To create and maintain an intuitive, massively simplified, fairly priced raffle and whitelist platform where non-technical business owners can seamlessly capture high-intent leads, grow their brands, never get left behind and still have enough money afterwards to keep the lights on.

We provide tools for creating raffles and whitelists, managing teams, and running cross-project collaborations — all connected to Discord and all deigned to speed up and economize your raffle/whitelist work!.

If you’re not using us, you’re not only wasting time, you're wasting money and effort!
