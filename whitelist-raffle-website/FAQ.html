Why 3DRaffles?

3DRaffles was designed out of a long bout of frustration with our competition products in all matters of the utility itself.

Marketing on the internet can be a confusing, horrifying and costly event if done incorrectly. However, when done correctly, Using raffles and whitelist events is an incredibly effective way to market products online, particularly for digital goods, limited-edition physical products, collectibles, and software.
By restricting access and gamifying the purchasing process, you shift the consumer dynamic from "being sold to" to "competing to buy."
The biggest advantage of this strategy is that it leverages human psychology (specifically scarcity), FOMO (fear of missing out), and exclusivity resulting in turning a standard product launch into a high-demand event.
But probably the greatest benefit raffles and whitelist events offer to the business man, is a closed, dedicated and "qualified" list that never stops growing of people who not only like your product or service, but know what it costs and enjoy using it!
That's litterally why they opted into your raffle! To buy your product or service and that's why they agreed to give you their email address so you can contact them again with new offers!
What better way is there to reach the few, qualified and dedicated buyers you need in an ocean of non interested people than using a utility that's both fun and exciting to participate in and ONLY APPEALS to your market?

What annoyed the crap outta me, was that the other guys either had no clue how critical raffling is to small and large business or they simple didn't give a crap.

    I was simply frustrated and angry with the lack of regard on the part of any of our competetors for: The quality of their product, The actual operation of their product, The tech support of their product and probably the most frustrating, The price they charged for the crap quality of their product.
So I built 3DRaffles for my business marketing needs and now I'm sharing it with you.

What is my public raffle title?

This is the place where you name your raffle as you want your entrants to see it. It's always a good idea to name your raffle including the theme and product and a date refference so as to keep your raffles easy to understand and identify when you're working with them today, next month or next year.

What is my Public Raffle Description?

This is the place where you describe for your entrants, what your raffle prize is or does for you!
It's always a good idea to give as good and broad a description as you can here because this is where you build excitement in your product and your raffle event.

How many people can I invite to my event?

The system currently has no limit to the number of people you can invite.
However, inviting an unusually high number can result in unusually long processing time after the event.

What should I put as a duration?

You raffle duration should be set in the number of hours you want it to run. That number is unrestricted however, It should be noted that your termination time should be planned with your event start date and time in mind so as to allow you some "in between time" that can be used for data distribution, contact and conversation with other collaborations partners and other work that may need to be accomplished PRIOR to the actual event your raffle is for.

How big can I make my banner image?

While there is no restriction on how big or small your banner is, it's important to understand that the space for it is fixed at 500'px wide by 100px tall. Your banner will be cropped to fit this space if it exceeds these limits and that may create a vastly different loking banner than you submitted.

How many people can win my raffle event?

You set that number in the data entry page. The system currently has no limit to the number of people who can win it's up to you and to your prize availability.

id="section-destination"How do I get my raffle results?

When you set up your raffle, the utility asks you to select a "server' and a "channel" where you want your results posted. At the end of your raffle the utility will automatically post your winners .csv file in that server and channel.
It's important to understand that whether you create a new channel or select an existing channel, you should consider restricting access to it to just your admin staff.

Can I require my entrants to do or get something to qualify?

Yes. Currently there is a short list of "entrant requirements" but as we become aware of new requirements submitted from our customer base, we'll add to it.

Can I post public messages or notes?

Yes. Currently we have a place where you can submit "Public Notes" regarding your project, you products, your details or anything else you feel you need your entrants to know prior to signing in.

Can I send a note to my winners?

Yes. Currently we have a place where you can submit "Winners Notes" regarding your congratulations, additional fulfillment instructions, contact info, or anything else you might want to convey to JUST your winners.

Can I include other url links to other sites or projects?

Yes. Currently we have a place where you can submit "Additional Raffle Info Links" regarding other places that the entrant can go to to get additional information, additional examples, additional education regarding your raffle or project.

I got an ERROR message that says "Payment required or expired - Launching is blocked"?

When this message comes up it's typically just a reminder thet you subscription has no been either paid ot renewed. You can still create your event but to launch it public, you'll need to either upgrade your version or make a payment to your account.

Can I seperate my raffles from my whitelists?

Yes. you simply select either Raffle or Whitelist from the choices in the "What type of event is this?" field and the system will refer to your event as either a raffle of a whitelist from then on.
